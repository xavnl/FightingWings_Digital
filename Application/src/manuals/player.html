Player manual

How to fly your aircraft with the page in a multiplayer (play by email) game: which tab to open, which button to press, which file to send to whom. The game rules are those of the Fighting Wings rule book (third edition) and its play aids; the page applies them and shows the rule numbers.

Before the first turn

  1. Open flight-table.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox (double-click). Keep the folders maps, counters and boxes next to it.
  2. The page keeps the game in your browser, one game per folder: always open the page from the same folder.
  3. When the referee's first game file arrives, click Import game file… (bar under the tabs, or Game tab) and choose it.
  4. In Game › Multiplayer (PBEM), set This copy is played by to your name. Everything else (players, referee, who flies which aircraft) is set by the referee and comes with the game file.

You can change only your own aircraft, and move it only when it is its turn. The referee's buttons are hidden.

The multiplayer bar

The bar under the tabs always shows the game turn, the current step and what you have to do, with the buttons you need now. Each time the page makes a file (your orders, the game file), the browser saves it in your Downloads folder. Send it by email to the person named in the file name.

A game turn

Each game turn has three phases (8.0): initiative, movement, combat. In the multiplayer game:

  1. every player declares the tailing of his aircraft; the referee rolls the initiative and sends the game file;
  2. the game file goes from player to player in the order of movement; each one moves his aircraft and may declare its attacks;
  3. the referee may ask for combat orders, then resolves defensive fire, the fixed gun attacks, the jettisons and the end of turn checks, and starts the next turn.

1. Initiative declarations

Open the Initiative tab. For each of your aircraft:

  • Tails: the aircraft it tails, if any (8.1.1). The list marks with ✓ the aircraft that can be tailed now (simple test or deflection test); otherwise the page says what is missing. A tailing aircraft moves just after the aircraft it tails.
  • Formation: the page checks your aircraft against its leader: fixed formation 2-1-3 (junior leaders 3-3-3) or flexible formation 6-6-6 (junior leaders 9-9-9), rule 8.5. A fixed formation moves together, first of all (class X). A flexible wingman may use his leader's initiative if it is higher: untick follow to keep his own.

Then click Send my orders and send the file to the referee.

Order of movement (8.1): fixed formations, then H and M class, then F and L class by load (overloaded first, then loaded L, then the others), and unsighted aircraft last; out-of-control aircraft first in their group. Initiative modifiers: Recruit −4, Green −2, Veteran, Ace, Hero +1 each, hero leader +1, severe damage −1, oil on canopy −2, own hero or ace shot down −1, enemy shot down in the last two turns +2. The lowest initiative moves first.

2. Your move

When the game file arrives with one of your aircraft to move, import it: the bar shows Moving: your aircraft. Select it (the chips under the tabs show the order of movement) and open the Maneuver tab.

Start of the move

  • Power (4.1): Military (max or more than 1/3), Emergency (the number after the slash on the card), Cruise (1/3 or less) or Idle (start speed −0.5). Loads, damage and cruise lower the max attainable speed.
  • Transition (6.1): stay in your attitude, or choose a pull (nose up for the pilot) or push (nose down) transition from the FATT. Each entry shows its rate, its steps, the end attitude and its FP code, for example Pull HT 2 → ISTC: 2/3V to All -1V RV after 2/3-. Entries your speed does not allow are greyed with the reason. « RV » = mandatory reversal, « NG » = negative G.
  • OC: the optional altitude change at the end of the move (6.1.3); End of move: optional reverse in vertical flight (6.4) or the free HFP of pure vertical flight.

The page shows the FPs to play (a carried half FP joins a new one), the VFPs allowed by the transition or by the Standard Altitude Change Table, the mandatory change (MC), the banking and side slip FPs and the turn rates you may use at your speed.

Each line shows its notation and the hex reached. On the map, the planned path is drawn in dots. Problems are written in red: fix them before going on. At the bottom, the page adds up the accel and decel points and shows the End Line: position, speed and carries at the end of the move.

Stall, spin, dangerous dive, compressibility

When a check is needed, a button appears at the top of the move: stall / spin (% ≥ 60 spins), spin recovery (≤ 50), dangerous dive (≥ 95 destroys), compressibility (≥ 60). The page rolls, with the modifiers shown, and flies the stall or spin move itself. All rolls are kept in Game › Dice rolls.

Finish

  1. Click Apply end of turn. If you used ET or NG BT rates (or BT with structural damage), the page rolls the overstress and black-out / red-out checks (5.6, 5.7). Undo apply goes back if you made a mistake.
  2. If you want, declare your attacks now (next section).
  3. Click Done: pass to next aircraft in the bar: the page saves the game file for the next player. Send it.

3. Your attacks

Open the Combat tab with your aircraft selected.

  • Fixed guns: choose the target (✓ = in your fields of fire), the gun groups, the burst (normal, half, long) and tick Tracking shot if allowed (10.1). The page shows the whole shot as it will be posted (range, deflection, odds, LH modifiers) and your chance to hit. Click Declare attack.
  • Defensive fire (bombers with rear gunners, 10.5): choose the target and the gunners of your formation that can fire, then Declare defensive fire.
  • Stores: Declare jettison drops your bombs in the Combat Phase (12.0).

The referee resolves defensive fire first, then the fixed gun attacks, then the jettisons. If he asks for combat orders, change or cancel your attacks (a target may have moved after you), then Send my orders.

Good to know

  • Save at the end of each phase: keep every game file you receive and send. If something goes wrong, the game can be taken up again from the last file.
  • The Log tab shows your moves and shots in the PBEM format, ready to copy into an email.
  • The ADC tab shows your aircraft's data card, with the line of its altitude band and the column of its speed highlighted.
  • The camera bar on the map: Overview, 3D view, Top, Center, and the aircraft models (Minibox or counters). North is up. Wheel: zoom; drag: turn the view; right-drag: move it.
  • Readable text (top of the panel) makes the figures larger and easier to tell apart.
