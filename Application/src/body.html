Order of movement

#AircraftClassD10 + pilot = initiativeNote

Lowest initiative moves first; ties are rolled again without modifiers (8.1). Order (8.1): X (fixed formations, side by side), H, M (side by side, lower side roll first), then F and L by load: overloaded F and L, loaded L, then normal L and normal or loaded F; unsighted aircraft last (8.1.2). U (out of control) aircraft move first in their class. A tailing aircraft or a wingman moves just after the aircraft it follows, in rank order. Modifiers: Recruit −4, Green −2, Veteran / Ace / Hero +1 each, immediate leader a Hero +1 (in formation), severely damaged −1, oil on canopy −2, each own Hero or Ace shot down −1, enemy shot down in the last 2 turns +2.

Formations (8.5)

        
Set in the Aircraft tab: each aircraft's formation leader, and on the leaders the formation type (fixed or flexible).
          Fixed: wingmen stacked with the leader, or on / behind his 03:00 or 09:00 line within 2 hexes, 100 ft and 30° (2-1-3); junior flight leaders 3-3-3 from
          a more senior leader (300 ft, at most 100 ft per hex away). Flexible: wingmen on / behind the 03:00 or 09:00 line within 6 hexes, 600 ft and 60° (same hex:
          300 ft and 30°, 6-6-6); junior flight leaders 9-9-9 from the squadron leader, not in his hex. Fixed formation limits: slips only, TT rate or less
          (EZ for H and M), upright level or shallow attitudes; else out of formation this turn and the next (a leader's flight becomes flexible for good).

Multiplayer (PBEM)

        

          
a referee and players exchanging files

        

        

          
          
          
          

        

        
Each turn: each player sends his initiative declarations (tailing, wingmen) to the referee; the referee rolls the
          initiative and sends the game file; the file then goes from player to player in the order of movement (each plots his aircraft's move and may declare its attack),
          and back to the referee, who may ask for combat orders, resolves the attacks and the end of turn checks, and starts the next turn.
          Save (export) the game file at the end of each phase and keep every file received and sent.
